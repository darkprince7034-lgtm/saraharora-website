Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

You can browse this website without creating an account or providing your email address, phone number, payment-card information, or other personal details.

1. Information We Do Not Request

This website does not ask visitors to create an account or submit an email address, phone number, postal address, password, payment-card number, bank details, or other financial information simply to browse the site or use its social links.

2. Chat Space

The Chat Space lets visitors send messages to Sarah and receive replies. Messages sent through the Chat Space are stored so that the conversation can be delivered and the visitor can receive a reply.

Visitors should avoid sending passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, financial details, or other sensitive personal information through the Chat Space.

3. Technical Information

Like most websites, basic technical information may be processed by the website infrastructure or hosting providers when a page is requested, such as IP address, browser information, device information, timestamps, and security-related request data. This information is used for normal website operation, security, and reliability.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The public homepage does not require an account or login and is designed to work without asking visitors for personal information. Third-party services used to load website resources, such as fonts or icons, may process technical request information according to their own policies.

5. External Links

This website contains links to Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X, Telegram, WhatsApp, and other third-party services. When you leave this website, the privacy practices of the destination service apply.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell visitor personal information. Information submitted through the Chat Space is used for operating that conversation and is not intended for advertising or resale.

7. Contact

If you have a privacy question about this website or the Chat Space, please use the Chat Space to contact Sarah.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
